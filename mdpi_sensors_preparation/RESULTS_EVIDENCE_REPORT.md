## 12. Claims that must not be made

- “First ever”, “first”, “novel for the first time”, “state-of-the-art” or “outperforms existing methods” — no systematic literature review was done and no published method was re-implemented.
- Any hardware, measured, silicon, prototype, real-sensor, energy, power or area result — none exists.
- PRrM as the proposed or primary method; or PRrM gate passes as the pre-registered decision.
- A behavioral-vs-ngspice difference computed as 0.656 − 0.649 (different grids); the transfer loss is 0.002.
- PRr phase spread “0.006–0.011”; the verified range is 0.005–0.011.
- No-monitor results in the ngspice domain (not run).
- That phase randomization adds benefit on top of the phase-robust descriptors (PRr − PR CI includes zero).
- That the method detects healthy records reliably (healthy layer recall is low).
- That the experiment is fully reproducible end-to-end from this package (simulator source and caches are missing).
- Generalization to unmodelled circuit effects (charge injection, op-amp slew, temperature, monitor nonlinearity).
- Any journal acceptance probability.
- “Deep learning” for a two-hidden-layer MLP.
