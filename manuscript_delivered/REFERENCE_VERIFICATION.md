## 1. How the references were checked

Two separate checks were made for every reference, and they are reported separately below.

1. **Bibliographic existence.** Authors, exact title, year, venue, volume, issue, pages or article number and DOI were taken from the Crossref record of the DOI (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`). Where no DOI exists, the publisher's own page was used: the JMLR page for scikit-learn, the arXiv record for Adam, and the ngspice documentation page for the ngspice-47 manual. The two IEEE standards were checked against their Crossref standard records (approval dates and DOIs).
2. **Support for the cited statement.** For each reference the abstract was read (from Crossref, or from OpenAlex where Crossref carries no abstract) and compared with the sentence that cites it. Standards were checked against their scope statements. **No full texts were read.** Where only the title and record could be checked (no abstract available), the citation is used only for a statement that the title itself supports; these cases are marked *title/record only*.

## 5. Claims removed or reworded because they could not be supported

- "No convergence failure mode" (earlier drafts and the protocol) was removed. The text now states what the grid search does guarantee (fixed evaluations, a value inside the grid) and what it does not (optimum outside the grid or below its resolution).
